About Lulu's Pet Store

Our Story

Lulu's Pet Store was founded by a group of lifelong animal lovers who believed that pet owners deserved a store that genuinely understood their pets' needs. What began as a small neighbourhood shop has grown into a trusted local destination for pet supplies, grooming, and expert advice.

Our Mission

To provide pet owners with quality products, reliable services, and knowledgeable support that helps every animal live a happy, healthy life.

Our Vision

To become our community's most trusted pet care destination, known for genuine care, expert service, and a deep love of animals.

Who We Serve

We proudly serve dog and cat owners, small animal and bird keepers, and anyone looking for trustworthy advice and quality products for their pets.
